### Post-Training Science for Supervised Fine-Tuning

- Summary: Baseten sweeps one lever at a time across Qwen3 (0.6-32B), Llama 3.1/3.2, and three Qwen3 MoEs up to 235B-A22B, on four real customer SFT datasets, for LoRA and full fine-tuning (970 of 1,008 dense cells eligible).
