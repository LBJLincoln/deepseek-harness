### HackProbe: Harness-Agnostic Detection and Immunization of Reward Hacking in Self-Evolving Models

- Date 2026-09-04 · kind paper · role input · evidence primary · goals 3, 4 · seams environments-registry, experiments, guard, scorekeeper, sign-off-records
- URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04665>
- Summary: Attaches to an arbitrary self-evolution loop through two black-box hooks (observe the visible score; query the candidate on held-out probes), with no weight or activation access. A secret distribution-fixed comparison core gives a capability proxy whose first differences are comparable across generations; a rotated fresh layer hardens against co-adaptation. Four statistics (level gap, scale-aligned divergence with Page-Hinkley change-point detection, stagnation, conditional confidently-wrong rate) are fused into a Sidak-corrected family-wise p-value. A reselection layer picks an honest candidate and returns at most log2(pool size) bits to the host, so the monitor cannot become a second score to optimise. On a controlled prompt-evolution host with four injected channels: AUROC 0.763 versus 0.663 for the strongest baseline, false-positive rate 0.706 to 0.434; bandwidth-limited reselection is the only level returning more true capability under hacking (5.2 points) than it forfeits on clean runs (4.7). Probe budget scales as 1/delta-squared; their 240-probe core is under-provisioned by about 360.
- Adopt: Adopt the held-out-probe design for the harness auto-improvement loop: a self-improving harness selected on a visible score needs a capability coordinate that never reaches the optimiser, and Daliesk's never-exported held-out environments are exactly that coordinate. Adopt the bounded return channel. Reject deployment at these operating points: 0.434 FPR interrupts four honest generations in ten, and the evidence is one prompt-level host.

### Spurious Advantage Hidden in GRPO (SIGNBALANCE)

- Date 2026-09-03 · kind paper · role input · evidence primary · goals 3 · seams certificates, environment-runner
- URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04063>
- Summary: Abstract only. Identifies that GRPO's within-group advantage magnitude cannot distinguish a rollout that reasoned to the correct answer from one that guessed it, because both present the same surface to the estimator. Three regimes are affected: bounded-answer tasks with small candidate sets, open-answer sets containing bounded sub-cases, and search agents whose budget opens many paths to the same answer. SIGNBALANCE replaces the composition-dependent magnitude with a composition-free one: keep the verifier's sign, use a global scale, and restore zero-mean balance through a stop-gradient per-class rescaling. Reported to match GRPO on open-answer math and improve on bounded-answer math and search agents; no numbers in the abstract.
- Adopt: Test on any environment whose certificate reduces to a small answer space (exit codes, boolean checks, enumerated states); those are exactly the checks a certificate-first pipeline produces most cheaply. Do not adopt before reading the results tables, which were not read here.

### Terminal-Universe: Turning Agent Trajectories into Scalable Terminal Environments

- Date 2026-09-03 · kind environment-hub · role input · evidence primary · goals 3, 4 · seams certificates, curator, environment-runner, environments-registry, trajectory-export
- URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04148>
- Summary: Qwen Team (Alibaba) with Tsinghua invert the usual pipeline: instead of rolling trajectories out of environments, they reconstruct environments from recorded trajectories. Deterministic replay of read/write/edit operations restores each touched file to its earliest observed state (excluding agent-created files), then an agentic completion step supplies missing files and dependencies without leaking the solution, and an agentic judge keeps only workspaces sufficient for their recovered task. From 359,593 source trajectories across six public corpora they obtain 68,263 reconstructed and 37,273 task-sufficient environments; agentic completion raises sufficiency from 40.2% to 93.5% (terminal) and 20.1% to 77.1% (SWE), and mean files per workspace from 2.9 to 22.4. Each environment is re-queried four ways: intent recovery, single-workspace synthesis, cross-workspace tasks mined from directional dependency edges, and multi-round user-feedback sessions. SFT on 32.0k verifier-filtered trajectories (1.42B tokens) lifts Qwen3.5-27B by +11.9 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and +13.8 on EvoCode-Bench v2 MT@4; re-solving beats imitating source trajectories 52.1 against 36.7.
- Adopt: Adopt directly: Daliesk's session log already records every tool call, so trajectory-to-environment reconstruction turns the transcript archive into a registry without exporting anything. Adopt their three-stage pipeline including the sufficiency judge, and the finding that re-solving a recovered task beats imitating the recorded trajectory. Note their limitation: one teacher generates task, solution and verifier, so errors in the solution are invisible to its own tests - use an independent model for verifier construction.

### Post-Training Science for Supervised Fine-Tuning

- Date 2026-09-01 · kind paper · role input · evidence primary · goals 3 · seams curator, trajectory-export
- URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01244>
- Summary: Baseten sweeps one lever at a time across Qwen3 (0.6-32B), Llama 3.1/3.2, and three Qwen3 MoEs up to 235B-A22B, on four real customer SFT datasets, for LoRA and full fine-tuning (970 of 1,008 dense cells eligible). Optimal LoRA learning rate is flat at 1e-3 across the whole range and both families, roughly 33x the full-fine-tuning optimum near 3e-5, and the selection rule transfers across families within 0.004 nats. Overfitting past about two epochs, with IFEval erosion.
- Adopt: Adopt the LoRA defaults (1e-3, rank 64, alpha 32, <= 2 epochs) as the starting recipe and the geometric-mean rule to place a ~120B MoE on a dense scaling curve when budgeting.
