# Verifiable environments, benchmarks, and evaluation practice, June to September 2026

Two facts organise the window. Public verifiers are unreliable often enough that gold-patch validation is a precondition, not a nicety. And the harness itself became a measured object: three benchmarks for harness engineering appeared within five weeks, and cost per solved task varies far more between harnesses than pass rate does.

## What changed

- **Environments as importable task trees.** Harbor separates task (instruction, sandbox image, verifier) from harness from sandbox; its Hub mirrors terminal-bench-3.0 (<https://huggingface.co/datasets/harborframework/terminal-bench-3.0>, 2026-08-21, Apache-2.0) and terminal-bench-science (2026-09-03) follow that layout. Terminal-Lego-15k (<https://huggingface.co/datasets/PrimeIntellect/Terminal-Lego-15k>, 2026-07-22) holds 13,825 task directories with instruction, task manifest, Dockerfile, and tests. SWE-rebench-V2-PRs (<https://huggingface.co/datasets/nebius/SWE-rebench-V2-PRs>, 2026-08-18, CC-BY-4.0) holds 126,300 pull requests; its gold-patch-verified cut keeps 6,272 of 32,079.
- **Verifiers fail at measurable rates.** Multi-SWE-RL-Verified (<https://huggingface.co/datasets/PrimeIntellect/Multi-SWE-RL-Verified>, 2026-07-08) keeps 2,232 of 4,703 rows and drops C++ entirely (0 of 449 passed gold-patch validation). terminal-bench-2-verified (<https://huggingface.co/datasets/zai-org/terminal-bench-2-verified>, 2026-08-24) fixes six tasks whose tests misjudged valid submissions. DeepSWE (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07946>, 2026-07-08) authors 113 original tasks with hand-written functional verifiers and reports an independent judge disagreeing 1.4 percent of the time against 32.4 percent for SWE-Bench Pro's inherited tests. Automated transcript analysis (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27518>, 2026-07-29, UK AISI and others) open-sources scanners that found verified flaws in CORE-Bench, CVE-Bench, and Terminal-Bench 2.0.
- **Hidden and tamper-aware grading.** Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench (<https://huggingface.co/datasets/IntelligenceLab/Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench>, 2026-08-19, 46 tasks) grades with hidden rebuild-from-artifact verifiers. trace-cheating-recall-500 (<https://huggingface.co/datasets/PrimeIntellect/trace-cheating-recall-500>, 2026-09-04) is 500 known-cheating SWE traces, a recall test for any tamper verdict.
- **Environments from trajectories.** Terminal-Universe (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04148>, 2026-09-03) reconstructs 37,273 task-sufficient environments from 359,593 trajectories; deterministic replay alone reaches 40.2 percent sufficiency, agentic completion 93.5 percent, and re-solving beats imitating the source trajectory. SETA (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10891>, 2026-07-12) releases 4,567 verified terminal environments with difficulty-adaptive evolution.
- **Measurement.** reliability@k (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14711>, 2026-08-11): common pass@k implementations substitute test-suite size for rollout count and inflate scores by 0.85 to 0.97 absolute. Deployment Decision Reliability (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11323>, 2026-08-11): the agent main effect is under 3 percent of variance, aggregate reliability collapses on the hardest quartile, and training-cell reliability correlates negatively with held-out reliability (r = −0.90). The Scaffold Effect (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22585>): harness choice moves paired pass rate by 0 to 8 points but tokens per solved task by up to 41.9 times. SWE-Bench ProMax (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09802>, 2026-08-10): scaffold choice alone moves one model from 21.8 to 41.2 percent.
- **Harness engineering, benchmarked.** HarnessOpt-Bench (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06301>, 2026-08-06) hides the test partition behind a trusted boundary and finds native harnesses win only 9 of 20 pairs. HarnessDev (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01437>, 2026-09-01) separates creation from evolution and finds evolution gains transfer only partially to a 630-task held-out split. EvoHarnessBench (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04280>, 2026-09-03) asks whether agents keep pace with a harness changing under them. Two leaderboards frame cost: Red Hat's coding-agent leaderboard (<https://huggingface.co/spaces/red-hat-emerging-technologies/coding-agent-leaderboard>, 2026-09-02) plots score against tokens and cost with a Pareto frontier; hlido-eu (<https://huggingface.co/spaces/hlido-eu/leaderboard>, updated 2026-09-06) scores shipped agent products with C2PA-signed evidence and longitudinal re-tests.

## What Daliesk adopts

1. Gold-patch validation on a restored fixture is a precondition of registry admission and of any certificate; the survival rate of every imported set is published with the set.
2. An adapter admits Harbor task trees (instruction, image, verifier) into the environments registry, so terminal-bench-3.0 and Terminal-Lego-15k enter without bespoke code.
3. trace-cheating-recall-500 is run against the tamper verdict and the recall is published before the verdict is trusted in a reward.
4. The scorekeeper records tokens per certified run, no-action turns, and a failure-category vector as first-class facts; no leaderboard row is published without cost.
5. Experiments report a held-out reliability estimate and a difficulty-conditional breakdown beside the paired-seed interval, and compute reliability@k over independent runs, never over tests.
6. Reconstruction of environments from the lab's own certified runs is the path to scale past hand-authored tasks; held-out environments never leave the lab because reconstruction never exports.
