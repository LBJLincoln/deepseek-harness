Running now

Loop

One row per bench loop iteration, in the order they ran, read from the machine-written loop/ledger.jsonl. The decision rule is fixed: promote adopts the candidate, reject and inconclusive keep the baseline, a fleet is only recorded. Adopting a candidate is a decision, not an edit — the composition change stays a human act.

Verdicts

Each card is one paired comparison: certificates per arm, the paired delta with its bootstrap interval, and the verdict rule read on that interval. Frozen pairs ran as one experiment; offline folds pair two separately recorded runs.

Product models on sealed tier 5

Plain single-model arms on the harness loop over the eight audited tasks, two repetitions at seed 2, three attempts; every sealed run of the same arm is pooled.

Cost of a certificate

Output tokens spent per certificate earned: each arm's output tokens over all its cells, divided by the certificates it produced.

Difficulty matrix

Certification rate per environment and route over sealed runs; a cell reads certified of run. Routes are the first rung, then the ladder, then the implementer.

Routing, read offline
