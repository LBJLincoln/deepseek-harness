# Improvement log

English | [中文](improvement-log.zh.md)

Every improvement iteration this harness has run, one row each, in the order they ran: the change that was proposed, the experiment or record that tested it, the paired reading that came back, and what the harness did about it. The rows are read from [the results note](../../.agents/notes/proposed/architecture/2026-09-08-hypothesis-program-results.md), the record paragraphs in [README.md](README.md), the fold files under [folds/](folds/), and, for an iteration a queue ran, the machine-written `loop/ledger.jsonl` beside them; [dashboard.html](dashboard.html) shows the same verdicts as a page.

The loop keeps a change only on a frozen paired experiment. The plan — both arms, the environment ids, the repetition count, the policy version and base seed, the four thresholds, and the caps — is digested before any cell runs, a plan whose two arms would resolve to different caps is refused outright, and the paired certificate-rate delta comes back with a percentile bootstrap interval; the verdict is `promote` when that interval's lower bound exceeds the plan's `minimumDelta`, `reject` when its upper bound is below zero, and `inconclusive` otherwise ([the experiments README](../../packages/improvement/experiments/README.md#statistics-and-verdict)). This bench sets `minimumDelta` to 0.05 and bounds every cell at 1,500,000 billed tokens and 1,200,000 ms ([cordis.yml](../../examples/headless-agent/tests/fixtures/proving-ground-bench/cordis.yml)). Cost is not in that rule: the result carries each arm's spend and token deltas beside the verdict and a reader weighs them, which is why no row below keeps a change whose certificates came at a worse price. An offline fold computes the same statistics over two separately recorded runs and is evidence, never a promotion, because its arms were not frozen together.

## What the loop still lacks

The pieces above run as a loop only with a person in it. Four parts are not automatic:

- **The proposal is authored outside the harness.** Nothing in [`packages/improvement/`](../../packages/improvement/README.md) forms a hypothesis or writes a plan; every plan is a hand-written JSON fixture under [`plans/`](../../examples/headless-agent/tests/fixtures/proving-ground-bench/plans/README.md), and so is every queue that orders them. A reading no longer has to be transcribed: a queued iteration writes its verdict, interval, and per-arm certificates into `loop/ledger.jsonl` as it records the run, and a row here reads that line — every row above predates the ledger and was read from the record by hand. The change a row proposes, and the sentence weighing its cost against its certificates, stay a person's.
- **Runs have a queue, and a schedule nobody has switched on.** [`pnpm run bench -- loop <queue>`](../../scripts/proving-ground.ts) runs an ordered queue of checked-in plans unattended: it records each run the way a hand-recorded run is recorded, reads the verdict back out of that record, applies a fixed rule — `promote` decides `adopt-candidate`, `reject` and `inconclusive` decide `keep-baseline`, a fleet decides `recorded` — and appends one machine-written line per iteration, writing a failed entry as such and continuing to the next. Nothing schedules that command or queues a plan from a verdict, the queue file is hand-written, and a decision is not an edit: the bullet below is still a person's work. Only the live district runs on a cadence, and it certifies three trivial tasks rather than testing a change. Four iterations ran on 2026-09-19 — the `openrouter-smoke` queue's one plan ([record](2026-09-19-bench-h1-fleet-openrouter-nex-smoke-t2/manifest.json)), the E3 replication from `nightly-tier5` ([record](2026-09-19-bench-e3-attempts-t5/manifest.json)), the preset pair from `presets-t5` ([record](2026-09-19-bench-e9-preset-craft-vs-plain-t5/manifest.json)), and the E7 second pair when `nightly-tier5` was resumed with `--from 2` ([record](2026-09-19-bench-e7-attempts-5-t5-2/manifest.json)) — as the ledger's first four lines, two of them concurrently on one subscription. The schedule exists outside the repository as a disabled account Routine that would run `nightly-tier5` in a fresh session at 02:00 UTC and push the records and the ledger ([README](README.md)); enabling it is the operator's decision, because every cell is billed to the operator's subscription, and no policy yet says what to queue next or when to stop.
- **A kept change is applied by editing the composition.** Nothing carries a promoted arm back into a default: a kept result is hand-edited into `cordis.yml` or an overlay. An arm cannot even name a composition — `EnvironmentRunRequest` carries no agent preset, so two compositions over one route stay incomparable ([the experiments README](../../packages/improvement/experiments/README.md#known-limitations-and-deferred-work)) — and the [`composition/manifest`](../../packages/components/components-manifest/README.md) event records what an agent had in play without being a way to change it.
- **Every paired reading is on one provider's models.** Every arm of every experiment above is the `claude-code` route on `haiku`, `sonnet`, or `opus`, run on the operator's own subscription, so each reading is about this harness on this bench through one vendor. The OpenRouter records of 2026-09-19 are fleets, not pairs, on free open-weight models under terms that admit training: the first two launches certified none of their six cells, the loop's first iteration certified both of its cells on `nex-agi/nex-n2.5-pro:free`, and the agentic fleet certified 16 of 18 across three models ([README](README.md)). No frozen pair has yet put a route other than the subscription in an arm. The `openrouter-pairs` queue holds the first such plan, `e10-openrouter-nex-vs-laguna-t2` — the two free models that certified every tier-2 cell in the agentic fleet, twelve pairs, sized to one day of the free tier's request allowance — and it has not run.
