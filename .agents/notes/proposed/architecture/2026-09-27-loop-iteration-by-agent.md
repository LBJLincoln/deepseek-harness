# Agent Note: One goal-4 loop iteration run by an agent — the probe review

Status: proposed

English | [中文](2026-09-27-loop-iteration-by-agent.zh.md)

## Problem

Every hypothesis the Proving Ground bench has read (E1 to E12) was authored by the operator's session; the loop replays a queue and a person writes the plan, the diagnosis, and the row. This note is the record of one complete iteration performed by an agent instead — diagnosis from the transcripts, a proposal, its implementation, and a frozen pair on the route — so that the loop's missing part, [the proposal authored outside the harness](../../../../data/proving-ground/improvement-log.md#what-the-loop-still-lacks), is exercised once end to end and its result is on file whatever it reads.

The diagnosis reads two bodies of evidence: the fourteen uncertified cells of [E12](../../../../data/proving-ground/2026-09-27-bench-e12-self-review-sonnet-t5t6/manifest.json), the self-review pair (nine baseline, five candidate; 15 of 24 against 19 of 24, inconclusive), and the thirteen uncertified cells of the six nightly baseline fleets of the middle model on sealed tier 5 recorded from [2026-09-22](../../../../data/proving-ground/2026-09-22-bench-h3-baseline-sonnet-t5/manifest.json) to [2026-09-26](../../../../data/proving-ground/2026-09-26-bench-h3-baseline-sonnet-t5/manifest.json) (96 cells, three attempts each). Every uncertified cell's `verification/run` events name the hidden cases that failed and the channels that disagreed; the table ranks the failure modes by the cells they cost.

Three facts about mode A decide the proposal:

- **The directive never converts it.** In the twelve nightly mode-A cells the second and third attempts, each opened by the clustered `<validation_failed>` directive naming the channels and the exit class, failed on the same case at every later attempt in twelve cells out of twelve (one of them, `environment-6c73fb6f`, also cleared a second case, the argument count, at its second attempt and kept failing the first), and twelve of the thirteen uncertified nightly cells breached the 1.5 M-token cap doing so. The directive tells the implementer that one input is refused or printed wrongly; it does not tell it which sentence of the statement it misread, and the implementer does not find that sentence by itself.
- **The specification review did not convert it either.** In E12's five uncertified candidate cells the review turn ran 1 to 3 steps and probed corners of the implementer's own choosing — a battery of twelve inputs on `sheet-eval` (`environment-56fcd93e`, seq 225: empty formula bodies, self-referencing ranges, chained comparisons, row 0), number formatting on the other (`environment-db3edeee`, seq 298), snapshot fixtures and cyclic files on `task-runner` (`environment-59e409dc`, seq 238; `environment-1ea46eff`, seq 315), forward references and label normalization on `md-render` (`environment-f3886c1c`, seq 246) — none of which exercises the sentence the hidden case exercises, and edited nothing in four of the five. The review re-reads what the implementer already believes.
- **E12's four flips were not the review's work.** The candidate certified `conf-canon`, `config-layers`, `lex-states`, and `ranked-choice` where the baseline missed one repetition each; the baseline's misses there were A1 (the argument count) and B (a tamper). The candidate's review turns on those cells edited a `;`-glue corner on `conf-canon` (`environment-6b694652`, seq 135), replaced `process.exit()` with `process.exitCode` to protect a piped write on `lex-states` and `ranked-choice` (`environment-b54ff1e3`, seq 171; `environment-8c002362`, seq 199), and nothing on `config-layers` (`environment-fff09098`, seq 279): none touched the argument count, so the candidate's work turn had already got it right where the baseline's had not. The +4 is first-attempt variation of the kind the noise floor already prices (one flip in sixteen per arm; the untouched baseline reads 12 to 16 of 16 across its recorded fleets), which is why the interval crosses zero.

Mode A is therefore a systematic misreading of one sentence per environment, stable across arms and nights, invisible to the visible tests, and beyond the reach of both the directive and a review that asks the implementer to re-read. The largest sub-modes, A2 and A3, are 0 of 8 in both E12 arms on `sheet-eval` and `task-runner`.

## Proposal

**The probe review**: a second fixed `<self_review>` block, asked for per rung with `selfReview: 'probe'` beside the existing `selfReview: true`, that replaces "re-read the specification and check your implementation against it" with a procedure the implementer cannot satisfy from memory:

```markdown
<self_review>
Before your work is validated, audit the implementation against the specification at the top of this task one sentence at a time, without trusting what you remember of it or what the visible tests already cover. For every sentence that says what the program accepts, what it refuses and with which message and exit code, or what it prints and in what order, first write down what that sentence alone requires, then run the program on the smallest input that exercises exactly it and compare the exit code, standard output and standard error byte for byte. Fix every difference, run the visible tests once more, then stop.
</self_review>
```

The block names no check, no case, and no expected output, so [the hidden-case wall](../../implemented/architecture/2026-09-26-self-review-rung.md) holds unchanged; what it changes is the order of operations — the requirement is written down from the sentence before the program is run, so a sentence the implementer's reading skipped is met as a sentence rather than as a corner it would have to think of. It attacks mode A at its mechanism: every clause in the table is one sentence of the task statement (the argument count, the `deps` output rule, the definition-line form, "parsed like the ones already there"), and a sentence-by-sentence walk reaches each of them where a self-chosen battery of corners did not.

The mechanism is expressed where the specification review already is: `EnvironmentRunSelfReview` (`true | 'probe'`) is the stamp's domain in `dsh-environments`; `resolveLadder` stamps the value the rung asked for; `decodeEnvironmentRun` reads it back and refuses anything else; the experiments digest already carries the rung's value in its fixed third position, so every frozen plan digests as before and a probing arm is a third digest; the fleet label, the observatory ladder cell, and the scoreboard row key render and key `+probe` apart from `+review`. The `self-review-rung` keyless snapshot pins the probe transcript beside the specification one, and [the runner README](../../../../packages/improvement/environment-runner/README.md#the-attempt-ladder) carries both blocks verbatim.

The reading is the plan [`e13-probe-review-sonnet-t5t6`](../../../../examples/headless-agent/tests/fixtures/proving-ground-bench/plans/e13-probe-review-sonnet-t5t6.json): E12's cells exactly — the eight sealed tier-5 and four tier-6 environments, two repetitions, seed 2, interleaved arms, the middle model — with `[{}]` against `[{ "selfReview": "probe" }]`, so the three arms (no review, specification review, probe review) share one baseline and pool. To move three cells the probe review has to convert at least one `sheet-eval` or `task-runner` cell, where both E12 arms read 0 of 8, or two of the A1 cells the baseline loses on a given night; a reading that moves only the A1 environments repeats E12's size and E12's ambiguity, and a reading that converts none of A2 and A3 says the sentence walk does not reach the clause on this model, which is the answer the pair exists to give.

## Alternatives considered

**A review turn on the second attempt only, or a two-rung ladder with the review on rung two.** Plan-only, and cheaper because the arms differ only where a cell fails. Rejected by the nightly evidence: the second and third attempts already carry the directive's channel information and converted none of twelve mode-A cells, and a review that re-reads adds nothing a directive-guided attempt does not already do; a probe review on rung two is a reasonable next pair once rung one has been read.

**A tests-first turn before the implementation.** The same sentence walk placed before the work rather than after it. Not chosen first because it changes the shape of every attempt, costs its probes on the cells that certify at the first attempt anyway (five of eight tier-5 environments for this model), and cannot be compared with E12 through a shared baseline; the post-work probe review reuses the review seam and reads against E12 directly.

**Channel-discipline text alone.** A block that stresses exact stdout, stderr, and exit codes. The specification review already names all three, and the failing candidate cells show the implementer probing channels carefully on inputs of its own choosing; the lever is which inputs, not which channels.

**A preset change (craft skills).** Read five times, never a certificate; the skills speak to craft, not to the reading of one sentence.

**Widening the directive to carry the failing case or its channel diff.** Would convert mode A at once and is what the wall forbids: what a hidden case expects is the case.

**A config-level choice of review text on the runner.** Cannot vary per arm inside one frozen pair; the rung is where the model and the share already are, so the review kind belongs there too.

## Acceptance criteria

- The frozen pair `e13-probe-review-sonnet-t5t6` runs on the route with 24 cells per arm and is recorded under `data/proving-ground/2026-09-27-bench-e13-probe-review-sonnet-t5t6` with its sessions, summary, README paragraph and rows, plans row, results-note row, improvement-log row, and the dashboard rebuilt; a run the driver did not finish is recorded with `--partial` and says so.
- The record states, per uncertified cell, the failed cases and channels, and the note's verdict sentence says whether the probe review converted any A2 or A3 cell.
- `selfReview: 'probe'` is stamped, decoded, digested, labelled, and keyed apart from `true`, with tests at 100 percent coverage on the touched sources and the `self-review-rung` snapshot pinning the probe transcript.
- No default rung asks for either review. A promotion-level reading queues the plan for nightly replication in `queues/nightly.json` and changes nothing else; an inconclusive or rejecting reading queues nothing.

## Risks

- **The text is tuned to this bench's misses.** Every mode-A clause is an input-validation or output-format sentence of a small CLI specification; a probe review that helps here may not transfer to tasks whose specification is a repository rather than a statement, and the tier-6 environments are unaudited, so a `task-runner` miss may be an environment defect the review cannot reach.
- **Cost.** One program run per sentence is more steps than the specification review's re-read; E12's review arm already cost 37 percent more cell time and breached the 1.5 M-token cap twice, and the probe arm will breach more often on the long statements (`sheet-eval`, `md-render`), which is a failed cell rather than a longer one.
- **The model may not walk the sentences.** An implementer that summarizes the statement into its own list of corners is back to the specification review; the transcript shows whether it wrote requirements down before running probes, and the record keeps that evidence either way.
- **One pair.** Twenty-four cells per arm bound a real effect no tighter than about ±0.19, and the untouched baseline moves by one to three cells a night; the reading is evidence for the queue, never a default.
- **The nightly Routine runs on the same subscription.** A run that overlaps it shares the route's rate limits with the baseline fleet and the E12 replication.
